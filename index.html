AgroBharat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

AgroBharat is a farmer-produce marketplace designed to help farmers share information about their available crops and connect with potential buyers. This policy explains how we handle personal information when people use our services, including our WhatsApp chatbot.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AgroBharat, we may collect your name, WhatsApp phone number, village, district, crop details, available quantity, expected harvest date, and messages you send to us. We may also collect information needed to manage buyer enquiries and marketplace requests.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to register produce listings, respond to enquiries, help farmers and buyers connect, communicate about buyer requests, operate and improve the service, and protect against misuse.

3. When Information Is Shared

AgroBharat may share relevant listing and contact information with potential buyers when needed to facilitate a marketplace connection. Information may also be processed by service providers that help operate our website, database, or WhatsApp communications. We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

4. WhatsApp

If you contact AgroBharat through WhatsApp, your messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. We use the information you provide to respond and operate the marketplace.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for marketplace operations, legitimate business purposes, and applicable legal requirements. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may ask to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and records we may need to retain. To make a request, contact us using the email below and include enough information for us to identify your request. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

7. Children's Privacy

AgroBharat is intended for people who can legally use the service. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as AgroBharat develops. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the date above.
